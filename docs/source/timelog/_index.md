Report on time logged on issues and merge requests.

## Synopsis

Time logs are the entries that time tracking records when you log time on an issue or merge request. Use `glab timelog list` to see who logged time, on which items, and the total, for a project or group over a date range.

To log time on an issue, use `glab issue update --spend`.
